# Interview Preparation Fundamentals

## Before the interview

Read the role description and identify the outcomes the team needs, not only the listed tools. Prepare a short example for each important requirement. For every example, note the situation, your responsibility, the actions you took, and the measurable result.

Review the projects and decisions on your resume. Be ready to explain trade-offs, alternatives you considered, what you personally delivered, and what you would improve now. Do not claim work or results that you cannot support with a specific example.

Prepare a concise introduction that connects your recent experience to the role. Keep it focused on relevant work, strengths, and the kind of problem you want to solve next.

## Technical questions

For a technical question, clarify assumptions before proposing a solution. Explain the approach in small steps, discuss complexity or operational trade-offs when relevant, and test the reasoning with a small example. If you do not know an answer, state what you know, identify the uncertainty, and describe how you would verify it.

When discussing system design, establish requirements and constraints first. Describe the main components and data flow, then cover reliability, security, observability, and the costs of the design. Avoid presenting a single architecture as universally correct.

## Behavioral questions

Use a concrete example rather than a general claim. Explain the context, your role, the actions you personally took, and the outcome. Include what you learned, especially when the result was mixed or the situation involved disagreement.

For conflict or failure questions, stay factual and respectful. Describe how you communicated, what changed as a result, and what you would do differently. Do not assign blame or imply that collaboration means avoiding disagreement.

## Questions for the interviewer

Ask questions that help you understand the work: how success is measured, what the team is focused on, how decisions are made, and what a typical collaboration pattern looks like. Tailor questions to details you learned during the conversation.

## Final preparation

Confirm the interview format, time zone, meeting link or location, and any materials you need. Leave time for questions and a concise closing summary of your relevant experience. Afterward, send a brief note that refers to a specific part of the discussion.
